Our Ministries

Places to belong, serve, and grow alongside this congregation.

Weekly

Summer in the Psalms

Our Sunday morning sermon series, moving through the Psalms in step with the calendar each week.

Weekly

Psalm 119 Study

A Wednesday night study working verse by verse through the longest chapter in Scripture.

Children

Children's Ministry

A nurturing environment where children grow in their understanding of God's love through engaging lessons, interactive activities, and supportive relationships with caring leaders — a safe space to explore faith and belong to God's family.

Students

Youth Ministry

A community where teenagers encounter God, grow in faith, and build lifelong friendships. Through relevant teaching and meaningful relationships with mentors, students are equipped to navigate life's challenges and live out their faith boldly.

Music

Music Ministry

Individuals use their God-given talents to lead others into the presence of God through music — uplifting melodies and skilled musicianship that create space to connect with God and respond in heartfelt praise.

Men

Men's Ministry

A community committed to equipping and empowering men in their spiritual walk through fellowship, discipleship, and service — growing in faith and building relationships that strengthen families and communities for Christ.

Women

Women's Ministry

A vibrant community dedicated to empowering women in their faith journey through events, programs, and gatherings — fostering fellowship, discipleship, outreach, and meaningful connection among women of all ages.

Nursery

Nursery

Warmly welcomes children ages 0–4 (or older if needed) with loving care from our dedicated nursery crew during Sunday services, with childcare also available for events beyond Sunday.

Care

David's Cradle

A compassionate ministry that transforms donated wedding gowns into Angel Gowns for preemies and full-term babies born asleep — each gown crafted with love and gifted at no cost to grieving families.

Care

Grief Support

Compassionate care for those navigating loss, led by Pastor Lopez and Nita Lopez, offering comfort and guidance through some of life's most difficult seasons.
