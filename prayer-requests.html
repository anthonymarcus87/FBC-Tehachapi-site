Setup note for whoever maintains this site: this form uses Web3Forms, a free service that emails form submissions — no server or database required. To activate it:

1. Go to web3forms.com and enter the prayer team lead's email address. They'll receive an Access Key by email.
2. Replace YOUR_WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY in this page's HTML (the hidden access_key field near the top of the form) with that key.
3. That's it — submissions will arrive by email from then on, free, with no submission limit.
